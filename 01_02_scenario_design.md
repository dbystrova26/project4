## 2. Scenario Designed for Paired Team (Tech Industry)

**Use Case:** AI-powered employee productivity monitoring and automated at-risk flagging

### Company: PulseWork

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **HQ** | Munich, Germany |
| **Size** | ~150 staff |
| **Clients** | ~600 enterprise clients across Germany, France, and the Netherlands |
| **Stage** | Series C closed — preparing for Series D |
| **Product** | Sells an AI-powered workforce analytics platform. Monitors employee activity (application usage, communication metadata, meeting attendance, task completion) and produces individual productivity scores. Automatically flags at-risk employees to HR and line managers when scores drop below threshold. |

### Company Problems *(Hidden — to be discovered through interview)*

- Core product is almost certainly a high-risk AI system under EU AI Act Annex III point 4 (AI in employment decisions) — never formally classified, no conformity assessment, no human oversight mechanism documented
- The at-risk flag is being used by clients to initiate disciplinary procedures and redundancies without any human review step — one Dutch client is currently facing a works council legal challenge the CEO has not disclosed to investors
- GDPR legitimate interest basis is undocumented, no DPIA has ever been conducted, and German works council co-determination rights under BetrVG are likely being violated at client sites
